## Executive Summary

At **IEEE AIoT**, we presented two works advancing **AI-native Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)** across aerial and human-centric sensing. The first introduces **ROISD**, which combines **RIS-assisted sensing** with **O-RAN–enabled intelligence** to enhance **UAV detection** through network-native control and scalable deployment. The second demonstrates an **AI-assisted composite ISAC waveform** for accurate **mmWave respiration pattern recognition** while preserving communication performance. Together, these presentations showcase a unified ISAC vision leveraging mmWave, RIS, and O-RAN for next-generation intelligent IoT systems.

## ROISD: RIS and O-RAN Assisted Intelligent Sensing for UAV Detection

**Authors:** Xiaochan Xue, Shucheng Yu, Saurabh Parkar, and Yao Zheng

### Key Points
- RIS-assisted sensing to enhance reflections/coverage for UAV detection  
- O-RAN architecture integration for intelligent sensing workflows  
- Detection pipeline considerations: sensing, fusion, and control/optimization  

## AI-Assisted Composite ISAC for mmWave Respiration Pattern Recognition

**Authors:** Xiaochan Xue, Saurabh Parkar, Shucheng Yu, and Yao Zheng

### Key Points
- Composite ISAC waveform design for simultaneous sensing + communication  
- mmWave respiration signal extraction and feature representation  
- AI/ML pipeline for respiration pattern classification and robustness
