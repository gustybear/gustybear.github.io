Yanjun Pan received the B.E. degree in Information Engineering from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, China in 2016. She earned her M.S. degree and Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Arizona, USA, in 2018 and 2021, respectively. She joined the Department of Computer Science and Computer Engineering at the University of Arkansas as an assistant professor in Spring 2022. Her current research interests include cybersecurity, wireless communications, wireless sensing, and network optimization, with emphases on wireless security, 5G-and-beyond communications, mmWave sensing, and cross-layer optimization.
