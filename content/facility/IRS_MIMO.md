## Equipment
- 2 Mi-Wave Omni-Directional Antennas, 26.4GHz – 40GHz Operating Frequency, Model# 267A-599
- 1 TMYTEK Horn Antenna, Dual-Polarization, 15-19 dBi Gain, 25-31 GHz Operating Frequency
- 1 TMYTEK 5G Beamformer, 16 Channels, 24.25 - 27.5 GHz Operating Frequency
- 1 Fieldfox N9951B VNA, up to 44 GHz

## 2x2 MIMO
This setup will help evaluate the impact of reconfigurable intelligent surfaces on MIMO performance at mmWave frequencies. It can support 28 GHz operation to reflect real-world 5G scenarios, characterize individual MIMO channel links with high accuracy, and accommodate and compare different reflector configurations, including RIS and traditional passive elements.
