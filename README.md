# Yao Zheng — Academic Website

Source repository for the academic website of **Yao Zheng**, Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the **University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa**.

**Visit the website:** [www2.hawaii.edu/~yaozheng](http://www2.hawaii.edu/~yaozheng/)

## About

My research focuses on intelligent wireless systems at the intersection of wireless communications, RF sensing, and artificial intelligence. Areas of interest include:

- Hardware–software–AI co-design
- Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC)
- Programmable wireless environments
- RF and millimeter-wave sensing
- Software-defined radio systems
- Digital twins

## Site contents

The website brings together my academic profile and the work of my research team, including:

- **Grants and projects:** funded research and ongoing research activities
- **Publications:** journal articles, conference papers, and related research outputs
- **Teaching:** courses and teaching materials
- **Team:** student and collaborator profiles
- **Facilities:** research equipment and laboratory resources
- **Events:** academic events and activities

## Repository

The site is built with Hugo and Hugo Blox. Pages and research content are maintained in `content/`, site configuration in `config/_default/`, and custom layouts in `layouts/`.
